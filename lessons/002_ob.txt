TYPE: CONCEPT
QUESTION: Why is the candle at the arrow marked as an order block, and why is the level drawn exactly there?
MY VIEW: The market initially moves strongly downward, establishing bearish structure. Price subsequently rallies back to the original bearish order-block area (black horizontal line and green rectangle). I would expect sellers to defend this zone. I would SELL.
Answer:
The OB is the last opposing candle before the displacement move.
In this example:
- The OB is the small bullish candle before the large bearish expansion.
- The large red candle is the displacement candle, not the OB.
For a bearish OB:
Last bullish candle before strong bearish displacement.

For a bullish OB:
Last bearish candle before strong bullish displacement.

The OB is a zone, not a single line.
Default definition:
- Body = main decision area.
- Full wick range = extended zone.
For conservative entries:
- Entry zone: candle body.
- Invalidation: beyond the full wick.
Yes, a valid OB should normally create meaningful displacement.
Strong OB requirements:
1. Opposing candle exists.
2. Strong displacement follows.
3. Displacement breaks structure (BOS/MSS) or removes liquidity.
4. Price leaves imbalance/FVG.
Without displacement, it is only a normal candle.
A bearish OB is invalidated when:
- Price closes decisively above the OB zone.
- Market accepts above the zone.
- The structure that created the OB is no longer valid.
A wick above does not automatically invalidate it.

The line should not be considered only the close.
For a bearish OB:
- The zone is the last bullish candle before bearish displacement.
- The upper boundary is normally the candle high.
- The lower boundary is normally the candle open/body area.
Preferred execution zone:
- Body of the candle.
- 50% of the candle range.
The wick is used mainly for invalidation.
For an OB invalidation:
- A wick above the OB does not invalidate it.
- A close above the OB body shows weakness.
- A close above the full wick shows strong invalidation.
Priority:
1. Close above wick = OB invalidated.
2. Multiple closes above body = OB likely invalidated.
3. Single wick above = liquidity sweep possible.
