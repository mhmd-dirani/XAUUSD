QUESTION: Why is this marked as an MSS? What do the circles mean and which candle confirms it?
MY VIEW: Price recovers from the lows, approaches the marked resistance area and eventually breaks above the MSS level with bullish displacement. Although a pullback is possible, the structural bias has shifted bullish. I would BUY.
Answer:
The blue circles mark important swing points/liquidity points used to identify the market structure shift.
They are not entries.
They represent:
- Previous protected high/low.
- Liquidity area.
- Structure reference.
Yes.
The MSS confirmation requires:
1. Price breaks the previous protected swing.
2. A candle closes beyond the MSS level.
3. Ideally displacement occurs.
The long green candle is the confirmation candle.
Require a close.
A wick above MSS is only a liquidity sweep.
Rules:
- Wick through MSS → not confirmed.
- Close above MSS → confirmed.
- Strong displacement after close → high-quality MSS.
In this example:
- The lower blue circle near the bottom represents the liquidity/sell-side sweep area.
- The upper structure level near the MSS line represents the protected high that must be broken.
The circle itself is not the confirmation. The important event is:
1. Liquidity is taken.
2. Price displaces upward.
3. Protected high breaks with a close.
